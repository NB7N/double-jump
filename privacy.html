Double Jump Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Double Jump is a checkers game for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information handled by Double Jump

Double Jump stores game-related information such as settings, wins, losses, streaks, selected board style, difficulty, and undo/game state locally on your device. The developer does not operate an account system and does not directly collect this local game data on a developer-controlled server.

Advertising and third-party services

Double Jump uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner ads and rewarded ads. Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and process information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and service operation. Depending on device and Google settings, this may include IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, app interactions such as app launches, taps and video views, diagnostic or performance information, and device or advertising identifiers.

Information handled by Google is governed by Google's own privacy policies and advertising technologies policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information for advertising

Data sharing

The developer does not sell personal information. Data processed through Google Mobile Ads may be shared with Google and its advertising or measurement partners as necessary to provide advertising, analytics, security, and fraud-prevention services.

Children

Double Jump is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising behavior and eligibility may also be governed by Google Play, AdMob, and applicable child-privacy requirements.

Security

Local game data remains on the device unless Android backup or other device services chosen by the user copy that data. Data transmitted by Google Mobile Ads is handled using Google's security practices, including encrypted transport as described in Google's documentation.

Your choices

You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID through Android settings where supported. You can also uninstall Double Jump to remove the app's locally stored game data from the device, subject to any device backup or restore services you have enabled.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Double Jump's features, advertising configuration, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions about Double Jump, use the developer contact information provided on the Double Jump Google Play store listing.
